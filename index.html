How It Works & Terms of Service

Simple 4-step workflow designed for rapid delivery and clear expectations.

STEP 01

Contact & References

Message Syloow on Discord (@sylomeann) or E-mail with character ref sheet, chosen tier ($15 or $20) and any pose preferences.

STEP 02

USD Payment

Slot is secured once upfront payment in USD is completed (via PayPal, Ko-fi or VGen). Work starts immediately.

STEP 03

Sketch & Approval

You receive an initial sketch/draft for review. Adjustments can be made freely at this phase before final lineart and coloring.

STEP 04

Final Delivery

Delivery of master high-resolution PNGs (plus transparent sticker files) via Discord DM or high-speed cloud drive link.

Will Draw:
  • • Original Characters (OCs) & Furry / Anthro
  • • Humans, Humanoids & Kemonomimi
  • • Fanarts (Anime, Games, Cartoons)
  • • YCH poses & Expressive Chibis/Stickers
  • • Sketches, WIPs & Gore/Blood (Soft)
Won't Draw:
  • • Heavy Mecha / Complex machinery
  • • Hyper-realism
  • • Hate speech, offensive or illegal themes
  • • Complex architectural backgrounds in $15-$20 slots

Due to the emergency nature of these slots, standard turnaround is 24 to 72 hours upon payment confirmation and reference approval.

In case of any unexpected delay, you will be notified immediately via Discord.

Revisions: 1 free revision is included during the rough sketch phase. Color adjustments after lineart completion are free. Major pose overhauls after sketch approval may incur a small fee.

Usage: For personal use, profile pictures, streaming avatars (Twitch/Discord), and personal sticker printing. Syloow reserves rights to showcase completed art in portfolios (watermarked).

Refunds: Full refund if work has not started. Partial refund depending on work completed.
